I am a PhD student in Psychology at the University of Edinburgh. 
My work combines cognitive and quantitative modelling and psychological research methods to study moral psychology.

I would summarise my work so far as studying the psychological mechanisms that allow us to make up or change our moral minds.
In my PhD thesis, I study analogical transfer as an underlying mechanism for moralization of neutral behaviours.
I am also developing an agent-based modelling framework showing the mechanistic role of moral values and how they change to produce political polarization.
Alongside my PhD, I work part-time as a research contractor with [Rethink Priorities](https://rethinkpriorities.org).

Before starting my PhD, I completed an MSc in Psychological Research (2024) and a BSc in Psychology (2023), both at the University of Edinburgh.

I am always keen to chat about research and collaborations, please reach out if you are keen to talk moral change!
