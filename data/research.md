All my work attempts to leverage psychological mechanisms to better understand or create moral or behavioural change.

### Moralization by Analogy

In this work, I test if neutral actions can become moral via analogical transfer with Drs Adam Moore, Matti Wilks, and Alex Doumas. 
Analogical reasoning has a huge literature supporting it as an induction strategy to discover new knowledge using what is already known from other domains.
Meanwhile, how moral change occurs continues to be a bit of mystery.
With this work, we propose and test whether analogical transfer can explain how actions become moralised at least in some instances.

### The Moral Mechanism of Political Polarization

In this work, we model polarization as an emergent phenomenon of social identity processes. 
Specifically, moral values act as signal that is emitted to show political group membership and used by others to infer the membership.
This work has been in progress since 2022 with Adam Moore and the final write up of the results of the original modelling framework is still in progress.
In collaboration with Nicolas Navarre, we have developed an alternative instantiation of the modelling framework, which we are looking for someone to expand one.

### Disgust and Dairy Consumption

Steve Loughnan and I investigated the role of disgust in breaking food-animal disassociation to deter dairy consumption. 
This work was a registered report and part of my undergraduate dissertation work.
I am not actively looking to lead work to expand on this, but I would be interested to collaborate.
