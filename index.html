Julie M. E. Pedersen

PhD Student · University of Edinburgh  |  Quantitative Researcher · Rethink Priorities

I am a second-year PhD student in the School of Philosophy, Psychology and Language Sciences at the University of Edinburgh. My research uses computational and behavioural methods to study how people form, signal, and change their moral and political beliefs — and how these processes give rise to polarisation at the population level.

Alongside my PhD, I work as a quantitative researcher at Rethink Priorities, where I analyse data and contribute to applied research on topics including AI risk communication, animal welfare messaging, and effective altruism.
