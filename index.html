Julie M. E. Pedersen

PhD Student · University of Edinburgh  |  Lab Manager · Human Decision-Making Lab

I am a second-year PhD student in the School of Philosophy, Psychology and Language Sciences at the University of Edinburgh. My thesis develops a framework called moralization by analogy — the idea that moral concern expands when people map the morally relevant features of already-protected groups onto novel targets — and uses computational and behavioural methods to test its predictions.

I also study how moral and political beliefs are signalled and inferred at the population level, and how these dynamics give rise to political polarisation. Alongside my PhD, I manage the Human Decision-Making Lab and work part-time as a research contractor with Rethink Priorities.

Before starting my PhD, I completed an MSc in Psychological Research with Distinction (2024) and a BSc in Psychology with First Class Honours (2023), both at the University of Edinburgh.
